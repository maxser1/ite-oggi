Italy today · Friday 27 November 2026

Yellow alert in eight regions, Dolomites pistes open on Saturday, strike on 4 December, Bacoli

A Friday with a yellow alert for rain and thunderstorms in the south and on the central Adriatic, a weekend that clears with colder air, the first Dolomiti Superski lifts on Saturday 28 in Cortina, Val di Fiemme, Plan de Corones and 3 Cime, the general strike of trains and planes between 3 and 4 December, the town of the day on the Campi Flegrei and the feast of St Andrew in Vinci on Monday.

The last weekend of November opens the winter season: skiing on the Dolomites starts on Saturday, the Alto Adige Christmas markets are already open and the art cities stay quiet until the Immacolata holiday. Today it still rains in the south and on the central Adriatic, then the weather improves with northerly winds and colder air.

Today 1 municipality honours Saint Laverio the martyr and 1 Saint Umile of Bisignano. The town of the day is Bacoli, at the tip of the Phlegraean peninsula: the land was formed by seven volcanoes aligned on a single axis, and the Romans built the Piscina mirabilis and the Cento Camerelle here. The nearest festival is on Monday 30 November in Vinci, for St Andrew.

The most useful news concerns anyone travelling next week: from 21.00 on Thursday 3 to 21.00 on Friday 4 December Trenitalia, Italo and Trenord staff strike, and on 4 December air transport too. Anyone who has to travel on those days books within the guaranteed time bands or leaves earlier.

Useful news for travellers

  • Yellow alert today in eight regions: rain and thunderstorms in Abruzzo, Basilicata, Calabria, Molise, Puglia and Sicily, hydrogeological risk also in Campania and Umbria; the weekend clears with cold and wind (Source: meteo.it, 26 November 16.39 (updated 17.20), on the Civil Protection Department's notice)
    According to meteo.it (26 November, 16.39, updated 17.20), the Civil Protection Department issued a yellow alert for rain and thunderstorms for Friday 27 November over six regions: Abruzzo (the lower Sangro, Tordino-Vomano and Pescara basins), Basilicata, Calabria (northern and central-northern Tyrrhenian side), Molise (coast), Puglia (Salento) and most of Sicily, including the Aeolian Islands, the Egadi, Ustica, Pantelleria and the Pelagie. The yellow alert for hydrogeological risk also covers the whole of Campania, from the Campanian plain to Naples, the islands, the Sorrento-Amalfi peninsula and the Cilento, as well as Molise, Puglia and Umbria (Chiascio-Topino, upper Tiber, Nera-Corno); for hydraulic risk, with possible overflowing of the main rivers, the Tyrrhenian side of Calabria and the Campanian plain with Naples, the islands, the Vesuvius area, the upper Volturno and the Matese are on yellow. No zone is on orange. For the weekend of 28 and 29 November the same site announces the last rain in the south, then a progressive improvement with high pressure building, strong northerly winds and falling temperatures. In practice: anyone in Sicily, Calabria or on the Campanian coast today puts off hikes and checks the links to the smaller islands; anyone leaving for the weekend finds improving but colder weather, with wind on the Adriatic and in the south.
  • Dolomiti Superski: the first lifts open on Saturday 28 November in Cortina d'Ampezzo, Val di Fiemme and Obereggen, Plan de Corones and 3 Cime Dolomiti; on 4 December Alpe Lusia, San Pellegrino, San Martino di Castrozza and Passo Rolle, on 5 December Civetta (Source: Corriere delle Alpi, 25 November)
    According to the Corriere delle Alpi (25 November), the winter season on the Dolomites pistes starts on Saturday 28 November with four areas of the Dolomiti Superski: Cortina d'Ampezzo, Val di Fiemme with Obereggen, Plan de Corones and 3 Cime Dolomiti in the Alta Pusteria. The other lifts come into operation from 4 December, when Alpe Lusia, San Pellegrino, San Martino di Castrozza and Passo Rolle open, and from 5 December with Civetta. Among the novelties of winter 2026-2027 the paper mentions new ten-seat gondolas, a new lift at Buffaure in Val di Fassa and one at Obereggen, while work continues on the link between San Martino di Castrozza and Passo Rolle. The consortium's president Andy Varallo said the priority is not to keep expanding the offer but to improve its quality. In practice: anyone who wants to ski this weekend picks one of the four open areas and, after Thursday's snowfall on the eastern Alps, sets off with winter tyres or chains on board; anyone aiming for Val di Fassa, Val Gardena or Alta Badia waits for the opening dates of the individual areas, which the paper does not give.
  • General strike on 4 December: Trenitalia, Italo and Trenord trains stop from 21.00 on Thursday 3 to 21.00 on Friday 4, air transport for the whole of the 4th, possible disruption on buses, metros and ferries (Source: meteo.it, 25 November 14.29 (updated 26 November 18.09))
    According to meteo.it (25 November, 14.29, updated 26 November at 18.09), the first date in December's strike calendar is the national general strike between Thursday 3 and Friday 4 December: Trenitalia, Italo and Trenord staff stop from 21.00 on Thursday 3 to 21.00 on Friday 4, with possible cancellations and changes to services; motorway staff from 22.00 on the 3rd to 22.00 on the 4th; air transport for the whole of Friday 4, from 00.00 to 23.59. For buses, trams, metros and sea links the arrangements are set locally, with possible disruption. The guaranteed time bands remain: for regional trains on weekdays from 6.00 to 9.00 and from 18.00 to 21.00, for flights from 7.00 to 10.00 and from 18.00 to 21.00 according to ENAC. Later in the month, on 14 December the Aviation Services ground staff at Fiumicino and Ciampino stop for 24 hours, on 15 December taxis across Italy from 8.00 to 22.00 and on 16 December Italo staff from 03.00 on the 16th to 02.00 on the 17th. In practice: anyone travelling by train or plane between 3 and 4 December checks the list of guaranteed trains on the operators' websites, chooses flights within the protected bands or moves the trip to 5 December, when the Immacolata long weekend begins.
