Italy today · Thursday 26 November 2026

Orange alert in Campania, 125 cm tide in Venice, snow on the Dolomites, San Nicolò d'Arcidano

A stormy Thursday with an orange alert until 14.00 over almost all of Campania and part of Molise, yellow in ten other regions, schools closed in Naples and Salerno, a 125 cm tide in Venice at 10.50 with the Mose barriers, snow down to the valley floors on the Dolomites, the town of the day on the Oristano plain and the feast of St Andrew in Vinci on Monday.

Late November is when Italy changes pace: art cities less crowded, spas and museums without queues, the Alto Adige Christmas markets open and the first ski lifts from Saturday. Today, though, is still a day of rain, wind and snow; from Friday the sun returns to the north with colder air.

Today 3 municipalities honour Saint Bellino of Padua and 1 Saint Leonardo of Porto Maurizio. The town of the day is San Nicolò d'Arcidano, on the plain between the Oristano area and the Medio Campidano: until 1864 it was simply called Arcidano, perhaps after the road to Monte Arci, where obsidian was quarried. The nearest festival is on Monday 30 November in Vinci, for St Andrew.

The most useful news is the orange alert in Campania until 14.00, with schools closed in Naples, Salerno, Sorrento and on the islands: hikes and ferry trips wait until the afternoon. In Venice the 125 cm tide is expected at 10.50. On the Dolomites snow is falling down to the valley floors: winter tyres or chains on board.

Saint of the day

Today is the feast of Saint Bellino of Padua, patron saint of 3 municipalities: Rovigo, Adria and San Bellino.

Today is the feast of Saint Leonardo of Porto Maurizio, patron saint of 1 municipality: Imperia.

Useful news for travellers

  • Weather alert today: orange over almost all of Campania and the Alto Volturno in Molise until 14.00, yellow in ten other regions; schools closed in Naples, Salerno, Sorrento, Ischia and Procida (Source: Regione Campania, Civil Protection, notice no. 758 of 25 November; ilmeteo.net (Meteored), 25 November 22.24; Adnkronos, 26 November 00.21)
    The Campania Civil Protection (notice no. 758 of 25 November) extended the orange alert for hydrogeological risk from 14.00 on Wednesday 25 to 14.00 on Thursday 26 November across all zones of the region except Alta Irpinia, Sannio and Tanagro, which stay yellow: landslides, rockfalls, mudflows and flooding in low-lying urban areas are feared, with strong south-westerly winds and rough seas until the same hour. According to ilmeteo.net (25 November, 22.24) the national notice also puts the Alto Volturno-Medio Sangro in Molise on orange and, for rain, thunderstorms or hydraulic risk, Abruzzo, Basilicata, Calabria, Emilia-Romagna, Friuli Venezia Giulia, Lazio, Marche, Puglia, Tuscany and Umbria on yellow; between Wednesday and Thursday more than 100 mm of rain is expected between southern Lazio and Campania, with peaks of 120 mm between Salerno and Avellino. Adnkronos (26 November, 00.21) reports schools closed today in Naples and Salerno and, among other towns, in Sorrento, Meta, Piano di Sorrento, Castellammare di Stabia, Pozzuoli, Bacoli, Ischia, Procida, Torre del Greco, Ercolano and Sapri. In practice: anyone in Naples, on the Sorrento peninsula or on the islands today avoids footpaths and roads along streams in the morning, checks ferry and hydrofoil departures before heading to the pier and puts off trips up Vesuvius and along the Amalfi coast until tomorrow.
  • Venice: 125 cm tide at 10.50 today, Mose barriers already raised yesterday and overnight, another lift possible this morning (Source: ilmeteo.net (Meteored), 26 November 05.00, on data from the City of Venice Tide Centre (bulletin of 25 November, 17.00))
    According to ilmeteo.net (26 November, 05.00), citing the 17.00 Wednesday bulletin of the City of Venice Tide Forecasting Centre, the tide has a first peak of 105 cm at 00.40 on Thursday, drops to about 65 cm around 05.00 and rises again to 125 cm at 10.50, a level the Tide Centre classes as a very high tide; the scirocco along the Adriatic and the rapid fall in pressure have raised sea levels along the coasts of Veneto and Friuli. The Mose system was already in operation on Wednesday morning, another lift was scheduled for Wednesday evening and one more is possible this morning; according to the Lagoon Authority's summary, closures since the start of 2026 stood at 39 against 27 in the whole of 2025. The seiches of the Adriatic may keep conditions favourable to high water for the rest of the week. In practice: anyone in Venice this morning checks the Tide Centre bulletin and the Actv notices before going out, because with the barriers raised the vaporetti can change route, and keeps boots or overshoes to hand for St Mark's Square in case the Mose is not activated.
  • Alps: up to 60 cm of snow above 1500 metres between last night and this morning, down to the valley floors on the Dolomites and in north-eastern Friuli, easing from the afternoon (Source: ilmeteo.net (Meteored), 25 November 22.24)
    According to ilmeteo.net (25 November, 22.24), after Wednesday's snowfall on the western Alps, where snow fell at times down to 600-700 metres in Piedmont, the most intense phase hits the central and eastern Alps between the night and the morning of Thursday 26, with up to 60 cm above 1500 metres and flakes that on the Dolomites and in the north-east of Friuli Venezia Giulia can locally reach the valley floors; from the afternoon the snowfall eases. In practice: anyone driving today to Cortina, Val Gardena, Val di Fassa or Tarvisio leaves with winter tyres or chains on board, checks the state of the passes before setting off and, if possible, delays the trip to the afternoon or tomorrow; anyone waiting for the lifts to open on Saturday 28 will find the pistes with fresh snow.
