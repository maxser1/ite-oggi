Italy today · Monday 16 November 2026

Yellow alert in ten regions, Cervara di Roma and South Tyrol's Christmas markets from 27 November

A rainy Monday along the Tyrrhenian side of Italy, the town of the day at the gates of the Monti Simbruini park, the feast of San Colombano in Bobbio on the 23rd and the dates of South Tyrol's five original Christmas markets.

The second half of November is the quietest stretch of the year for travelling in Italy: art cities without queues, low hotel prices. The price is the weather: today a new front brings rain and thunderstorms to the Tyrrhenian regions of central and southern Italy, with showers and clear spells in the North. A morning for museums and churches, with a look at the regional bulletin.

The calendar remembers Saint Fidenzio of Padua. The town of the day is Cervara di Roma: a mountain village in the Monti Simbruini, with the historic centre built against the ruins of the medieval fortress and the church of the Visitation, in local stone, with a 15th-century portal. The first document naming Cervara dates from 884. The nearest feast is San Colombano in Bobbio, on Monday 23 November.

The most useful news: South Tyrol's five original Christmas markets run from 27 November to 6 January, and Bolzano opens on Thursday 26 at 5 pm. Anyone who wants to avoid the crowds books now and picks a weekday in the first week.

Useful news for travellers

  • Yellow weather alert today in ten regions, rain and thunderstorms on the Tyrrhenian side of the Centre-South (Source: Fanpage.it, 15 November, 3.42 pm)
    For Monday 16 November the Civil Protection has issued a yellow alert in Abruzzo, Basilicata, Calabria, Campania, Emilia-Romagna, Lazio, Marche, Molise, Tuscany and Umbria: across the whole of Lazio and Campania, and for specific zones in the other regions. Flood risk on the Tyrrhenian side of Calabria, on the southern coastal basins of Lazio and in Campania on the Sele plain and upper Cilento, the Sorrento-Amalfi peninsula, the Sarno and Picentini mountains, Tusciano and upper Sele; thunderstorms in Abruzzo (Marsica, upper Sangro, Aterno), Basilicata, Tyrrhenian Calabria, Molise and Umbria; landslide risk also around Rome, in Naples and on its islands, in the Bologna and Romagna mountains, in Tuscany (Bisenzio and Pistoia Ombrone, lower Valdarno, Arno coast) and in two zones of the Marche. According to the Air Force forecast quoted in the article, widespread bad weather on the Tyrrhenian side of central and southern Italy, in Sardinia, Umbria and the inland areas of the Marche and Abruzzo, spreading in weaker form to Puglia and western Sicily; in the North scattered showers on the Alps, fog and mist in the valleys.
  • South Tyrol's original Christmas markets from 27 November to 6 January, Bolzano opens on the 26th at 5 pm (Source: suedtirol.info (IDM South Tyrol), original markets page and Bolzano 2026/27 event page, read on 16 November)
    The five original Christmas markets of South Tyrol, in Bolzano, Merano, Bressanone, Brunico and Vipiteno, run from 27 November 2026 to 6 January 2027, according to the official page of IDM South Tyrol. The event page for Bolzano gives the opening ceremony on Thursday 26 November at 5 pm, stalls open from 10 am to 7 pm, closure on 25 December and reduced hours on 31 December and 1 January; from 26 November to 23 December Piazza del Grano also hosts the Solidarity Market. Visitors arriving by train can enter a prize competition for the whole duration of the markets.
