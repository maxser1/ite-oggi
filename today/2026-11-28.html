Italy today · Saturday 28 November 2026

Yellow alert in the south and on the Adriatic, Trenitalia confirms the strike of 3 and 4 December, Songavazzo

A Saturday with a yellow alert for thunderstorms and hydrogeological risk in Abruzzo, Calabria, Marche, Molise, Puglia and Sicily, Trenitalia's official confirmation of the strike from 21.00 on Thursday 3 to 21.00 on Friday 4 December with buses stopping on the 4th, the town of the day on the Clusone plateau and the novenas in Pagani from Sunday.

The last Saturday of November opens the winter season: skiing starts on the Dolomites, the Alpine Christmas markets are open and the art cities stay quiet until the Immacolata holiday. Today, though, the weather is still unsettled in the south and along the Adriatic, with thunderstorms and wind.

Today 1 municipality honours Saint Giacomo della Marca. The town of the day is Songavazzo, in the Bergamo area, on a terrace of meadows and woods between the Clusone plateau and the Borlezza valley: the 1911 bridge over the Valleggia stream is described as the first lightweight reinforced-concrete bridge in Europe. The nearest festival begins tomorrow in Pagani, with the novenas of the Immacolata from 29 November to 7 December.

The most useful news concerns anyone travelling next week: Trenitalia confirms on its website the strike from 21.00 on Thursday 3 to 21.00 on Friday 4 December, and on the 4th local bus and tram staff also stop for 24 hours. Anyone who has to travel on those days picks a guaranteed train or leaves earlier.

Saint of the day

Today is the feast of Saint Giacomo della Marca, patron saint of 1 municipality: Monteprandone.

Useful news for travellers

  • Yellow alert today in six regions: thunderstorms in Abruzzo, Calabria, Molise, Puglia and Sicily, hydrogeological risk also in the Marche, hydraulic risk on the Calabrian coasts (Source: meteo.it, 27 November 17.26 (updated 28 November 3.04), on the Civil Protection Department's bulletin)
    According to meteo.it (27 November, 17.26, updated 28 November at 3.04), the Civil Protection Department issued a yellow alert for thunderstorms for Saturday 28 November over Abruzzo (the lower Sangro, Tordino-Vomano and Pescara basins), Calabria (central-southern and southern Tyrrhenian side, southern Ionian side), Molise (coast), Puglia (Tavoliere and the lower Candelaro, Cervaro and Carapelle basins, Gargano and Tremiti, lower Fortore) and Sicily (north-east on the Ionian and Tyrrhenian sides with the Aeolian Islands, central-northern Tyrrhenian side). The yellow alert for hydrogeological risk covers the same zones and the Marche as well; the one for hydraulic risk, with possible river flooding, is limited to the three Calabrian sides. No zone is on orange. In practice: anyone in Calabria, around Messina, on the Gargano or on the Abruzzo and Molise coast today puts off mountain and riverside hikes and checks the links to the Aeolian and Tremiti islands; anyone in the centre and north has no alert in force.
  • Trenitalia confirms the FS Group staff strike from 21.00 on Thursday 3 to 21.00 on Friday 4 December; on 4 December local bus and tram staff also stop for 24 hours, with buses and FrecciaLink at risk (Source: Trenitalia, strike page of the official website, read on 28 November)
    On its strike page, Trenitalia announces the strike of FS Italiane Group staff from 21.00 on 3 December to 21.00 on 4 December. The guaranteed Frecce and Intercity trains are listed in a table that can be downloaded from the same page and also appear when searching for a single train on lefrecce.it; for regional trains the list of guaranteed minimum services of each region applies. Trenitalia invites anyone holding a ticket for a train that is not guaranteed not to give up the journey: in the event of changes or cancellations it informs customers and explains how to continue, change the ticket or request a refund, through the app, the website, Infomobilità, the freephone number 800 89 20 21 and ticket offices. The same page reports for 4 December 2026 the 24-hour strike of staff of companies covered by the local public transport contract: bus services may be changed or cancelled, there are no guaranteed slots for FrecciaLink, and services may be affected before the start and after the end of the strike. In practice: anyone travelling by train between Thursday evening and Friday evening picks a train in the table or moves the departure to Saturday 5, the start of the Immacolata long weekend; anyone relying on replacement buses or FrecciaLink on 4 December makes other arrangements.
