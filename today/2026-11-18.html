Italy today · Wednesday 18 November 2026

Gale-force winds over half of Italy, Giffoni Valle Piana and Friday's general strike

A Wednesday of strong winds and yellow alerts in six regions, the town of the day in the Monti Picentini, the feast of San Colombano in Bobbio on the 23rd, Friday's general strike with Atac's guaranteed hours, air transport stoppages on 21 and 22 November and the first Dolomiti Superski slopes on the 28th.

The third week of November is a transition: art cities empty and affordable, slopes still closed, Christmas markets about to open. Today, though, the wind sets the agenda: the Civil Protection reports strong to gale-force winds across the centre and south, Emilia-Romagna and Liguria, yellow alerts in six regions and sea storms on exposed coasts. Hikes are postponed, ferries and cable cars need checking.

No Italian town celebrates its patron saint today. The town of the day is Giffoni Valle Piana, in the province of Salerno, at 196 metres at the foot of Monte Licinici in the Monti Picentini Regional Park: home of the Giffoni Film Festival, founded in 1971, and of the Giffoni hazelnut. The nearest festival is San Colombano in Bobbio, Monday 23 November.

The most useful news concerns Friday 20: a 24-hour general strike, with Rome's buses and metro guaranteed only until 8.29 and from 17.00 to 19.59, and trains halted from 21.00 until 20.00 on Saturday. Anyone flying at the weekend checks the flight: on Saturday 21 Enav stops from 13.00 to 17.00, on Sunday 22 it is Malpensa and Venice.

Useful news for travellers

  • Yellow alert today in six regions and winds up to severe gale across the centre and south (Source: MeteoWeb, 17 November, 16.26; Fanpage.it, 17 November, 16.20)
    For Wednesday 18 November the Civil Protection has issued a yellow alert for parts of Valle d'Aosta, Emilia-Romagna, Umbria, Marche, Abruzzo and Molise. Since late on Tuesday 17 a warning has been in force for strong to gale-force westerly winds over Valle d'Aosta, Piedmont, Abruzzo, Campania, Molise, Puglia, Basilicata, Calabria and Sicily, reaching severe gale on all Apennine sectors, with possible sea storms along exposed coasts. During the day isolated and scattered showers are expected over the central and western Alps, northern and Apennine Tuscany, Umbria, Lazio, western Sardinia, Abruzzo, Molise, Campania, western Basilicata and Calabria; strong to gale-force south-westerly winds across the whole centre and south, Emilia-Romagna and Liguria, with foehn on the Alps; rough seas on the Ligurian Sea, the Sea and Strait of Sardinia and the central Tyrrhenian.
  • Friday 20 November 24-hour general strike: Rome buses and metro guaranteed only until 8.29 and from 17.00 to 19.59, then trains stop from 21.00 (Source: Atac Roma, strike notice for 20 November; Qualitytravel.it, 1 November)
    The general strike called by Cgil and Cobas for Friday 20 November also halts local public transport. In Rome, Atac guarantees services on the whole network from the start of service until 8.29 and from 17.00 to 19.59; service is not guaranteed from 8.30 to 17.00 and from 20.00 to the end of daytime service, nor are metro A, B/B1 and C runs scheduled after midnight. Night buses are not guaranteed on the night of 19 to 20 November, but they are on the night of 20 to 21. The lines of Autoservizi Troiani/Sap, Autoservizi Tuscia/Bis and Atr are also affected. From 21.00 on Friday 20 to 20.00 on Saturday 21 a 23-hour national rail strike by CUB Trasporti and SGB follows, affecting Trenitalia regional and long-distance services, with guaranteed trains listed by the companies. On Saturday 21 staff at several local operators strike for the whole day, including GTT in Turin, Trentino Trasporti, EAV in Campania, SAD in South Tyrol, Ferrovie del Sud Est and Ferrotramviaria in Puglia.
  • Flights: Enav stops nationwide on Saturday 21 from 13.00 to 17.00, 24-hour strike at Malpensa and Venice on Sunday 22 (Source: Missionline.it, 26 October; Qualitytravel.it, 1 November)
    On Saturday 21 November staff of Enav, the air traffic control agency, strike nationwide for four hours, from 13.00 to 17.00, called by FILT-CGIL and UGL-TA. On Sunday 22 November the strike by Enav staff at Milan Malpensa lasts 24 hours, from 00.01 to 24.00 (RSA FAST-CONFSAL-AV); on the same day the security staff of Sicuritalia IVRI at Venice Marco Polo airport stop from 00.00 to 23.59 (FISASCAT-CISL). Anyone flying in those hours checks the flight status with the airline and the lists of guaranteed flights published by the authorities; in Venice it is wise to arrive at security well in advance.
  • Dolomiti Superski opens on 28 November with Plan de Corones, Val di Fiemme and San Martino di Castrozza; Bolzano's Christmas market lights up on the 26th (Source: Dolomiti.it, 6 October; SiViaggia, 9 October)
    The 2026/27 Dolomiti Superski season starts on Saturday 28 November with Plan de Corones, Val di Fiemme/Obereggen and San Martino di Castrozza/Passo Rolle. On 4 December Alta Badia, Val Gardena/Alpe di Siusi, Val di Fassa/Carezza, Rio Pusteria-Bressanone, Alpe Lusia-San Pellegrino and Arabba/Marmolada follow; the dates for Cortina d'Ampezzo, 3 Cime, Civetta and the Sellaronda are still being updated. Two days earlier, on Thursday 26 November at 17.00, the Bolzano Christmas Market is due to open in Piazza Walther and runs until 6 January, with the weekend market in Piazza Mazzini from 27 November to 20 December and historic trains to Bressanone and Merano on 29 November and 8, 13 and 20 December.
