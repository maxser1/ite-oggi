Italy today · Monday 23 November 2026

Gale winds in the South, Sala Baganza, San Colombano in Bobbio, Florence Marathon on Sunday 29

A Monday of gale-force winds over the southern regions, Sardinia and the coasts of Tuscany and Lazio with a yellow alert in Emilia-Romagna and Umbria, the town of the day at the gates of Parma between the Rocca Sanvitale and the Boschi di Carrega, the feast of San Colombano in Bobbio, the Florence Marathon on Sunday 29 November starting and finishing in Piazza del Duomo, and the Christmas markets opening this week from Merano to Bolzano and from 1 December in Rome and Milan.

The last week of November changes Italy's season: on Thursday 26 the Merano Christmas market opens, on Friday 27 those of Bolzano, Bressanone, Brunico and Vipiteno, on Saturday 28 the first big ski resorts, from Livigno to Plan de Corones. In the cities it is the time for museums without queues, with one eye on the calendar: Friday 4 December brings a general strike.

Today 16 towns celebrate Pope Saint Clement I and 9 Saint Colombano of Bobbio. The town of the day is Sala Baganza, at the gates of Parma: the Rocca Sanvitale, rebuilt from 1477, keeps Renaissance frescoes and thirteen rococo rooms, with the wine museum in its cellars; to the north-west begin the 2,600 hectares of the Boschi di Carrega. The nearest feast is today itself, San Colombano in Bobbio; on Tuesday 24 Roccalbegna lights the Focarazza di Santa Caterina bonfire.

The most useful news is the wind: today gale gusts over Sardinia, the coasts of Tuscany and Lazio and the southern regions, rough western seas until the evening. Anyone with a ferry to the islands checks the crossing before setting off; anyone in Florence on Sunday 29 finds the centre taken over by the marathon.

Saint of the day

Today is the feast of Pope Saint Clement I, patron saint of 16 municipalities, including Velletri, Valdagno, Magione, Pellezzano, Bellinzago Novarese and 11 more.

Today is the feast of Saint Colombano of Bobbio, patron saint of 9 municipalities, including San Colombano al Lambro, Bobbio, San Colombano Certenoli, Vernasca, Santa Giuletta and 4 more.

Useful news for travellers

  • Wind today: gale gusts over Sardinia, the coasts of Tuscany and Lazio and the southern regions, very rough seas; yellow alert in Emilia-Romagna and Umbria (Source: MeteoWeb, 22 November, 4.13 pm)
    For Monday 23 November the Civil Protection Department issued a yellow alert for parts of Emilia-Romagna and Umbria and a severe weather warning extending Saturday's: from late afternoon on Sunday 22, strong to gale-force westerly winds over Molise, Campania, Puglia, Basilicata, Calabria and Sicily, above all on exposed coastal and mountain areas, with heavy swells along the coasts. Today's surveillance bulletin forecasts strong to gale-force north-westerly winds over Sardinia, the Tuscan and Lazio coasts and the South, with severe gale gusts over eastern Sardinia, easing in the second half of the day; rough to very rough seas in all the western basins, with the swell dropping from the evening. Light scattered rain from Valle d'Aosta and the Marche down to Calabria and the Gargano. On Tuesday 24 the wind stays strong at first over Sicily, Calabria and Salento, then drops during the morning; snow above 800-1,000 metres in Valle d'Aosta and alpine Piedmont. In practice: anyone with a ferry today to Sardinia, Sicily, the Aeolian or Tuscan islands checks the crossing with the company before leaving and avoids seafronts and exposed coastal roads.
  • Florence: on Sunday 29 November the 42nd Florence Marathon, already sold out, starts and finishes in Piazza del Duomo and crosses the Cascine, Ponte Vecchio and Piazza della Signoria (Source: Florence Marathon, official site, 2026 race page)
    The 42nd Florence Marathon is run on Sunday 29 November 2026, the last Sunday of the month, starting from Piazza del Duomo and finishing in Via dei Pecori, beside the cathedral. The 42.195 km course leaves the centre along Via Martelli, Via Cavour and Piazza San Marco, follows the viali to Porta a Prato, reaches the Cascine park, comes back along the Arno by Lungarno Serristori and Ponte San Niccolò at the halfway point, passes Lungarno Aldo Moro and Viale Malta and returns to the centre at km 40 from Lungarno Vespucci, with Ponte Vecchio and Piazza della Signoria in the final stretches. Entries are closed: the official site shows sold out. The Expo with bib collection is at Stazione Leopolda, Viale Fratelli Rosselli 5, where the pacemakers can be met on Saturday 28 November; refreshment points every 5 km. Anyone with a train or flight from Florence that day leaves early: the course takes up the centre and the viali for the whole morning, and for closures and bus diversions it is worth checking the city council's website in the days before.
  • Christmas markets: Merano opens on Thursday 26, Bolzano, Bressanone, Brunico and Vipiteno on Friday 27, Canale di Tenno on Saturday 28; Piazza Navona in Rome and Piazza Duomo in Milan from 1 December (Source: The Wom Travel, updated 11 November)
    According to The Wom Travel's calendar, updated on 11 November, the Merano market opens on Thursday 26 November at 5 pm along the Lungo Passirio promenade and stays until 6 January; Bolzano inaugurates in Piazza Walther on Thursday 26 at 5 pm and opens its stalls from 27 November to 6 January, daily from 10 am to 7 pm; Bressanone, Brunico and Vipiteno open on 27 November until 6 January. In Trentino the Canale di Tenno market runs from 28 November to 20 December, 9.30 am to 6 pm; Trento has been open since 20 November, 10 am to 8 pm, and Aosta since 21 November, 10 am to 8 pm in Piazza Chanoux. In Rome the Piazza Navona market is scheduled from 1 December to 6 January, in Milan the Piazza Duomo market from 1 December to 6 January; in Florence the Weihnachtsmarkt in Piazza Santa Croce is already open until 20 December, 10 am to 10 pm on weekdays and until 11 pm from Friday to Sunday. On the first weekend South Tyrol is full: anyone arriving by train in Bolzano or Merano books early, and anyone coming by car uses the park-and-ride car parks.
