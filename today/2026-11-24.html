Italy today · Tuesday 24 November 2026

A one-day lull, Lana among the apple orchards, the Focarazza in Roccalbegna, Alpine snow from tomorrow

A Tuesday of easing wind and fog on the plains before the storm of Wednesday 25 and Thursday 26, with up to 90 millimetres of rain on eastern Liguria, northern Tuscany and the Prealps and heavy snow on the Alps, the town of the day in South Tyrol's largest apple district, the Focarazza di Santa Caterina bonfire in Roccalbegna tonight and the Sterntaler Christmas market in Lana opening on Friday 27 in the Capuchin garden.

Today is a day of respite: the wind that battered the South and Sardinia drops from the morning, with sunny spells and dense fog on the Po plain. It is the day to use for getting around, because from tomorrow a low over the upper Tyrrhenian brings two days of rain and snow on the Alps.

Today 2 towns remember Saint Firmina of Amelia. The town of the day is Lana, in the province of Bolzano, South Tyrol's largest apple district: the Residenza Larchgut has housed the South Tyrolean Fruit Growing Museum since 1990, and Foiana has Mayenburg castle. Tonight Roccalbegna, in the Maremma, lights the Focarazza di Santa Caterina bonfire.

The most useful news is Wednesday and Thursday's bad weather: up to 90 millimetres of rain on eastern Liguria, northern Tuscany and the Prealps, snow on the Alps from 700-1,200 metres, gusts of 80-90 km/h. Anyone driving across the Alps leaves today or waits until Friday, with chains in the boot.

Saint of the day

Today is the feast of Saint Firmina of Amelia, patron saint of 2 municipalities: Civitavecchia and Amelia.

Useful news for travellers

  • Bad weather on Wednesday 25 and Thursday 26: up to 90 millimetres of rain on eastern Liguria, northern Tuscany and the Prealps, snow on the Alps from 700-1,200 metres and down to 500 in the Cuneo area, gusts of 80-90 km/h (Source: ilmeteo.it, 23 November, 6.30 pm; ilmeteo.net (Meteored), 23 November; neveappennino.it, 23 November)
    According to ilmeteo.it, updated on 23 November at 6.30 pm, on Wednesday 25 November a low between the Ligurian Sea and the upper Tyrrhenian brings rain from the morning: 30-50 millimetres in 24 hours in the North and on the Tyrrhenian side down to Campania, with peaks of 70-90 on eastern Liguria, northern Tuscany, the Lombard and Venetian Prealps and the Friulian foothills; Sardinia and the central Adriatic stay around 10-20 millimetres. On the Alps heavy snow from 700-1,200 metres, with accumulations of up to 60-80 centimetres at altitude; sirocco and libeccio winds with gusts of 80-90 km/h and seas of 3.5-4.5 metres on the Ligurian Sea, the Sardinian sea and the central-northern Tyrrhenian, with highs of 6-9 degrees in the North. Meteored (ilmeteo.net, 23 November) says snow is possible down to about 500 metres in the Cuneo area and, by Friday 27, accumulations locally above 20 centimetres on the western Alps, above all in Valle d'Aosta; on Thursday 26 the low stays put with more scattered rain on the Tyrrhenian regions, the North-East and the Apennines. For neveappennino.it (23 November) on Thursday snow falls between 1,200 and 1,700 metres on the eastern Alps and the central-southern Apennines, from the Tuscan-Emilian range to the Sibillini, the Gran Sasso and the Maiella; from Friday 27 to Saturday 28 a very gradual improvement, with colder air on the Adriatic side at the weekend. In practice: anyone crossing the Alps by car between Wednesday and Thursday carries chains and checks the passes before leaving; anyone in Liguria or Versilia on Wednesday avoids exposed coastal roads and keeps away from streams.
  • Lana: the Sterntaler Christmas market opens on Friday 27 November in the Capuchin garden, 23 stalls open on weekends until 31 December, 10 am to 7.30 pm (Source: IDM South Tyrol, suedtirol.info, 2026 event page; Merano and surroundings, merano-suedtirol.it, Christmas in Lana page)
    The Sterntaler market in Lana, in the garden of the Capuchin monastery at Via Andreas Hofer 2, opens on Friday 27 November and runs until 31 December 2026, but only on selected days: 27 to 29 November, 4 to 8 December, 11 to 13 and 18 to 20 December, always from 10 am to 7.30 pm with the food stalls open until 9 pm; on 24 December from 10 am to 2 pm and on 31 December from 10 am to 8 pm with food only. According to IDM South Tyrol (suedtirol.info) there are 23 stalls with South Tyrolean crafts, local food and hot drinks; the Merano and surroundings tourist office adds live sheep, pony rides, a puppet theatre, the Sterntaler bakery, concerts and a charity lottery with tickets at 2 euros to be redeemed by 20 December, in aid of the Vinzenzverein of Lana. A Christmas post box sends postcards, with franking handled by the tourist office. Lana is a few kilometres from Merano, with stations at Lana-Postal and Merano-Sinigo about 3 kilometres from the centre: a way to see a village market on the same weekend the big South Tyrolean ones open.
