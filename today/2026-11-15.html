Italy today · Sunday 15 November 2026

Yellow weather alert in twelve regions, Fiesole above Florence and a week of strikes

A rainy Sunday across central and southern Italy and Sardinia, the town of the day on the hill of Fiesole, the feast of San Felice in Nola and the strike calendar for 16 to 22 November.

Mid-November is still the quietest season for travelling in Italy: no queues at museums, low hotel prices, Christmas markets not yet open. Today, though, rain sets the pace in central and southern Italy and in Sardinia, while the North is more settled: indoor visits in the morning, walks in the afternoon, when the showers become more isolated.

The calendar remembers Saint Leopold III of Babenberg. The town of the day is Fiesole, in the province of Florence: a double hill at 295 metres above the Arno and the Mugnone, with Piazza Mino da Fiesole at its centre, the convent of San Francesco on the summit where the Etruscan acropolis stood and, lower down, the Roman theatre. The nearest feast is today: San Felice in Nola.

The most useful news is the strike calendar: in Naples ANM buses and metro stop on Monday 16 from 11 am, Friday 20 brings a general strike, and from 9 pm on Friday until Saturday 21 evening the railways stop. Anyone travelling between 20 and 22 November checks the guaranteed trains on their operator's website the day before.

Saint of the day

Today is the feast of Saint Leopoldo III of Babenberg, patron saint of 1 municipality: Follonica.

Useful news for travellers

  • Yellow weather alert today in twelve regions, rain and thunderstorms in the Centre-South and Sardinia (Source: Fanpage.it, 14 November, 4.53 pm)
    For Sunday 15 November the Civil Protection has issued a yellow alert in Abruzzo, Basilicata, Calabria, Campania, Emilia-Romagna, Lazio, Marche, Molise, Puglia, Sardinia, Tuscany and Umbria, for specific zones rather than whole regions. Flood risk on the Bologna and Modena plains, on the Tyrrhenian side of Calabria, on the Campania coast from Sorrento to the Sele and on the southern coastal basins of Lazio; thunderstorms in southern Tuscany (Maremma, Giglio), Umbria, Puglia, western Sardinia, Molise, inland Abruzzo and Basilicata; landslide risk also around Rome, Naples and its islands, the Sorrento-Amalfi peninsula and the Marche. Widespread showers in the morning with possible heavy thunderstorms in Lazio and Campania; more settled weather in the North, with snow on the western Alps above 1,300-1,500 metres; more isolated showers in the afternoon. Rough to very rough seas.
  • Strikes from 16 to 22 November: ANM in Naples on Monday, general strike on Friday 20, trains stop overnight 20-21 (Source: QuiFinanza, 15 November, 5.00 am)
    On Monday 16 in Naples, ANM staff (buses, metro, funiculars) strike from 11 am, called by Usb, Orsa, Faisa and Confail; the end is given as 2 pm by QuiFinanza and 3 pm by other sources. On Friday 20 a general strike of all sectors, called by Cgil and by grassroots unions for the whole day: trains and flights are excluded from the Cgil call, but city buses and metros are at risk; demonstrations in more than a hundred cities. From 9 pm on Friday 20 until Saturday 21 evening (9 pm according to QuiFinanza, 8 pm according to Virgilio) a national strike of rail transport and local public transport: each company sets its own guaranteed services. On Saturday 21, from 9 am to 5 pm, RFI infrastructure staff in the Verona area also stop. On Sunday 22 a 24-hour strike by ENAV air traffic controllers at Milan Malpensa, with guaranteed slots 7-10 am and 6-9 pm, and by Sicuritalia security staff at Venice airport.
