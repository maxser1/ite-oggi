Italy today · Saturday 14 November 2026

Orange alert on the Emilia plains, Capannoli and a bus strike in Bari

A rainy Saturday across Emilia-Romagna, Tuscany and Campania, the town of the day in the Valdera, buses stopped in Bari and an ANM strike in Naples on Monday.

Mid-November is still the quietest time to travel in Italy: no queues at the museums, low hotel prices, Christmas markets not yet open. The weather, though, rules this weekend: after a short break in the morning, rain returns from the afternoon over the north-west and Emilia-Romagna, with thunderstorms on the Tyrrhenian coasts of central Italy. Anyone out and about today should plan indoor visits.

The calendar remembers Saint Veneranda and Saint Giovanni of Tufara. The town of the day is Capannoli, in the province of Pisa, on the hill road that runs down from Volterra into the Valdera: the historic centre sits on the highest point, and Villa Bourbon-Baciocchi, now owned by the town, was for a time the home of Elisa Bonaparte, Napoleon's sister. The nearest festival is San Felice in Nola on Sunday 15.

The most useful news is the Civil Protection alert: orange for flood risk on the Bologna and Modena plains, yellow in other parts of Emilia-Romagna, Tuscany, Marche, Umbria and Campania. Anyone crossing the region by car or train should check the regional bulletin before leaving. In Bari today AMTAB buses run only between 5.30 and 8.29 am and between 12.30 and 3.29 pm; in Naples on Monday 16 ANM services stop from 11 am to 3 pm.

Saint of the day

Today is the feast of Saint Veneranda, patron saint of 7 municipalities, including Acireale, Avola, Santa Venerina, Fermignano, Grotte and 2 more.

Today is the feast of Saint Giovanni of Tufara, patron saint of 2 municipalities: Foiano di Val Fortore and Tufara.

Useful news for travellers

  • Weather alert today: orange on the Bologna and Modena plains, yellow in five regions (Source: Meteo.it, updated 14 November, 6.01 am)
    For Saturday 14 November the Civil Protection has issued an orange alert for flood risk in the Pianura bolognese and Pianura modenese zones of Emilia-Romagna. Yellow alert for flood risk on the rest of the Emilia plain, on the Tuscan basins of the Serchio, Arno and Bisenzio (including Florence, Lucca, Pistoia, the Valdarno and the coast) and in the Marche; yellow for landslide risk on the Emilia and Romagna Apennines, the Garfagnana, Umbria (Chiascio-Topino, upper Tiber, Nera-Corno) and most of Campania, including Naples, the islands, the Vesuvius area and the Sorrento-Amalfi peninsula. The weather worsens again from the afternoon and stays unsettled for the second half of the weekend.
  • Bari: 24-hour AMTAB bus strike today, Saturday 14 (Source: Borderline24, 13 November, 2.44 pm)
    AMTAB staff are on strike for the whole day, called by Uil, Cgil, Cisl and Ugl. Buses and services are guaranteed only between 5.30 and 8.29 am and between 12.30 and 3.29 pm; at other times (from the start of service to 5.29 am, 8.30 am to 12.29 pm and from 3.30 pm to the end of service) runs depend on how many staff join, which at the previous company-wide strike was 23.6 per cent. Offices, workshops and car parks are closed all day.
  • Naples: ANM stoppage on Monday 16 November from 11 am to 3 pm (Source: Money.it, 2 November)
    A four-hour strike by ANM staff called by Filt Cgil, Fit Cisl, Uilt Uil, Ugl Fna and Faisa Cisal: buses, trams, metro Line 1 and the funiculars may stop between 11 am and 3 pm. The same calendar still lists the general strike on Friday 20, the rail strike from 9 pm on the 20th to 8 pm on Saturday 21, and ENAV air traffic controllers on Saturday 21 from 1 to 5 pm. Calendar as of 2 November: check the company's website the day before.
