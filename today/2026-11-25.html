Italy today · Wednesday 25 November 2026

Orange alert in Emilia-Romagna and Lazio, Alpine snow, Mose in Venice, Acerra for St Catherine

A wet and windy Wednesday with an orange alert on parts of Emilia-Romagna and Lazio and yellow in seven regions, snow on the Alps down to 500 metres in the north-west and winter-equipment checks in Trentino, a 110 cm tide in Venice at 10.15 with the Mose barriers scheduled, the town of the day in the hinterland of Naples and the feast of St Catherine in Novi Ligure.

Today and tomorrow are the hardest days of the week for getting around: a low over the Ligurian Sea brings rain to the north and to the whole Tyrrhenian side down to Campania, snow on the Alps and rough seas. From Friday the north improves, and Sunday is the most settled day.

Today 27 municipalities honour Saint Catherine of Alexandria and 3 Saint Mercurio of Caesarea. The town of the day is Acerra, in the province of Naples: under the old stables of the Castello dei Conti the remains of a Roman theatre from the 2nd century BC were found, and since 2023 the castle has housed the Maas archaeological museum. The nearest festival is today in Novi Ligure, for St Catherine.

The most useful news is the orange alert on parts of Emilia-Romagna and Lazio, with thunderstorms from the morning down to Campania. Anyone crossing the Alps today or tomorrow carries chains: in Trentino the checks are active. In Venice the Mose barriers are scheduled for the 110 cm tide at 10.15.

Saint of the day

Today is the feast of Saint Catherine of Alexandria, patron saint of 27 municipalities, including Scandiano, Guastalla, Locri, Bertinoro, Paceco and 22 more.

Today is the feast of Saint Mercurio of Caesarea, patron saint of 3 municipalities: Serracapriola, Seminara and Toro.

Useful news for travellers

  • Weather alert today: orange on parts of Emilia-Romagna and Lazio, yellow in Tuscany, Abruzzo, Umbria, Campania, Molise and the rest of Emilia-Romagna and Lazio, with thunderstorms and gusts from the morning (Source: ilmeteo.it, 24 November 18.48; MeteoWeb, 24 November 18.01)
    The national Civil Protection notice, reported by ilmeteo.it on 24 November at 18.48 and by MeteoWeb on 24 November at 18.01, sets for Wednesday 25 November an orange alert on parts of Emilia-Romagna and Lazio and a yellow alert in Emilia-Romagna, Tuscany, Abruzzo, the remaining sectors of Lazio and the whole of Umbria, Campania and Molise, for hydrogeological and hydraulic risk. Rain arrives in the morning over Lazio, Campania and the western parts of Abruzzo and Molise, with heavy showers, frequent lightning and gusts of wind; scattered to widespread rain also over northern Piedmont, eastern Liguria and northern Tuscany. The outlook bulletin for Thursday 26 gives rain over Lombardy, the Triveneto, southern Campania, western Basilicata, Tyrrhenian Calabria and southern Puglia, strong southerly winds over Puglia and the Ionian coasts and very rough conditions on all seas. In practice: anyone in Rome, Naples or along the Lazio and Campania coast today avoids underpasses and roads along streams during the storms and checks the regional pages for local alerts; anyone planning to drive through the Emilian Apennines or the valleys around Frosinone postpones if possible.
  • Alps: snow today down to 500-700 metres in Piedmont and 800-1200 metres elsewhere, 50-80 cm at altitude by Thursday; winter-equipment checks in Trentino, bora and sunshine in the north from Friday (Source: ilmeteo.it, 24 November 18.30; ilmeteo.net (Meteored), 23 November; MeteoWeb, 24 November 21.09)
    According to ilmeteo.it (24 November, 18.30) the low over the Ligurian Sea brings 48 hours of heavy rain to the north and to the Tyrrhenian side down to Campania, with peaks of 70-90 mm in 24 hours between eastern Liguria, northern Tuscany, the Prealps and the Friuli foothills and between southern Lazio and Campania; on the Alps snow falls today at 500-700 metres in Piedmont and 800-1200 metres elsewhere, with 50-80 cm by Thursday and peaks of 90-110 cm above 1800-2000 metres. Meteored (ilmeteo.net, 23 November) gives 12-20 cm for Sestriere, Limone Piemonte, Courmayeur, La Thuile and Cervinia and over 45 cm at the highest levels of Trentino-Alto Adige. For MeteoWeb (24 November, 21.09) the heaviest snowfall in Trentino comes between tonight and Thursday morning above 1300-1500 metres, with wet snow down to 700-1000 metres in sheltered valleys; the Province is setting up checkpoints with local police, state police and Carabinieri to verify winter equipment, which is compulsory (winter tyres or chains on board) on provincial roads, state roads and the Brenner motorway until 15 April 2027, and advises keeping journeys to a minimum. On Friday 27 the sun returns to most of the north except Emilia-Romagna, with showers and snow at 700-1100 metres on the central Apennines and in the south and bora and north-easterly winds of 60-80 km/h; Saturday 28 brings a cold bora with highs 3-5 degrees below average, Sunday 29 is the most settled day. In practice: anyone crossing the Brenner, Fréjus or Mont Blanc today or tomorrow drives on winter tyres or with chains on board and checks road conditions before leaving; those who can move the crossing to Friday or Sunday.
  • Venice: 110 cm tide today at 10.15 with the Mose barriers scheduled, another 110 cm peak at 0.10 tonight; Christmas lights on since 20 November in Piazzetta San Marco (Source: City of Venice, Live page, notice of 24 November and news of 20 November; Tide Centre, note of 23 November)
    The City of Venice Live page reports, in its notice of 24 November, a high tide of 110 cm at 10.15 on Wednesday 25 November with the Mose system scheduled to be raised, then a low tide of 10 cm at 17.20 and a new high of 110 cm at 0.10; on 23 November the Tide Centre convened its technical panel with Ispra and Cnr-Ismar for the tide events linked to the 25-26 November storm. On the same page the City describes the lighting, on Friday 20 November, of the 15-metre fir between the columns of Marco and Todaro in Piazzetta San Marco, with lights also on the arches of the Procuratie, in the shopping streets, in Mestre and on the islands. In practice: anyone in Venice today checks the Tide Centre bulletin and the Actv notices before moving around in the morning and overnight, and keeps boots or overshoes to hand in the lowest areas in case the barriers are not raised.
  • Milan: the Oh Bej! Oh Bej! fair around the Castello Sforzesco from 7 to 10 December, 8.30 to 21.00, free entry (Source: YesMilano (City of Milan), event listing 2026)
    The listing on YesMilano, the city's tourism portal, gives this year's Sant'Ambrogio fair around and behind the Castello Sforzesco from 7 to 10 December, daily from 8.30 to 21.00, with free admission: stalls of florists, wrought-iron, copper and brass craftsmen, toys, sweets, honey, roast chestnuts and the firunatt, strings of smoked chestnuts. In practice: the fair falls on the days of the Immacolata long weekend, so anyone who wants to sleep in Milan between 7 and 10 December books now; the long hours, 8.30 to 21.00, make it possible to go early in the morning or after dinner, when it is less crowded.
