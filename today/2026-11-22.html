Italy today · Sunday 22 November 2026

Orange alert in Tuscany, Thiesi among the nuraghi, Malpensa strike 1-5 pm, skiing from 28 November

A Sunday of bad weather with an orange alert over northern Tuscany and yellow in seven regions, the town of the day in Sardinia's Meilogu among domus de janas and nuraghi, the feast of San Colombano in Bobbio on Monday 23, the ENAV strike at Milan Malpensa cut to four hours and security staff stopped at Venice, trains at risk between 3 and 4 December, and the big ski resorts opening on 28 November and 4 December.

The last week of November brings the first scheduled snow: Cervinia has been open since October, on Saturday 28 Livigno, Courmayeur, Monterosa, Tonale and Plan de Corones open, and on 4 December Bormio, Sestriere, Alta Badia and Val Gardena follow. In the lowlands it is the season for art cities without queues and for spas, today with an umbrella.

Today five towns celebrate Saint Cecilia. The town of the day is Thiesi, in the Meilogu, in the province of Sassari: a place of cheese and prehistory, with the four domus de janas of Mandra Antine and the three-basin altar of Monte Mesu; the primary school keeps a fresco by Aligi Sassu, who lived here as a boy. The nearest feast is San Colombano in Bobbio on Monday 23 November.

The most useful news is the Civil Protection alert: orange from 6 am to 6 pm over Lunigiana, Garfagnana, Versilia and the Serchio valley, orange for wind and storm surges on the Tuscan coast and islands from the afternoon, yellow in six other regions. Anyone flying from Malpensa today checks flights between 1 and 5 pm; anyone leaving from Venice gets to security early.

Saint of the day

Today is the feast of Saint Cecilia, patron saint of 5 municipalities: Villafranca Padovana, Acquasparta, Pradamano, Caresanablot and Escolca.

Useful news for travellers

  • Weather alert today: orange over northern Tuscany from 6 am to 6 pm, orange for wind and storm surges on the Tuscan coast from the afternoon, yellow in seven regions (Source: MeteoWeb, 21 November, 4.40 pm and 4.07 pm)
    For Sunday 22 November the Civil Protection department issued an orange alert for hydrogeological risk over the northern basins of Tuscany and a yellow alert in Friuli Venezia Giulia, Emilia-Romagna, Liguria, Tuscany, Umbria, Lazio and Molise. From the early hours, scattered to widespread rain over Liguria, Tuscany and Emilia-Romagna, with heavy showers and lightning; strong to gale-force westerly winds first over Piedmont and Sardinia, then over Tuscany, Emilia-Romagna, Marche, Umbria, Lazio and Abruzzo, with severe gale gusts on the western Alps of Piedmont and the Adriatic side of the Apennines, and heavy seas on exposed coasts. In Tuscany the regional operations room set the orange alert from 6 am to 6 pm over Lunigiana, Versilia, Garfagnana, the Serchio valley and Val di Lima, yellow all day over Mugello, the Bisenzio and the Ombrone pistoiese, orange for wind on the central coast and the islands from 3 pm to midnight and for storm surges from 6 pm to midnight; in Florence yellow for wind from 3 pm, with gusts up to 50-60 km/h on the plain. On Monday 23 north-westerly winds stay strong over Sardinia, the Tuscan coast and the South, easing. Anyone travelling today avoids exposed coastal roads, seafronts and the ferries to the Tuscan islands in the afternoon and evening, and checks services before setting out.
  • Flights today: at Milan Malpensa the ENAV strike has been cut to four hours, from 1 pm to 5 pm; at Venice Marco Polo security staff stop for 24 hours (Source: ENAV, note of 21 November via Prima Pagina News; Trasporti-Italia, 20 November; Viaggi-USA, updated 18 November)
    ENAV has announced that the strike by air traffic services staff at Milan Malpensa on Sunday 22 November, called by FAST Confsal AV, lasts four hours, from 1 pm to 5 pm, no longer the 24 hours first announced; the essential services required by law are guaranteed, and the protected bands of air transport, 7-10 am and 6-9 pm, fall outside the strike hours. According to the calendars of Viaggi-USA and Telepass, updated between 18 and 20 November, at Venice Marco Polo the Sicuritalia IVRI security staff stop for the whole day (Fisascat-CISL); Trasporti-Italia, checking the Strike Guarantee Commission's calendar on 20 November, however found the Malpensa action as the only active stoppage in air transport. The national ENAV strike of Saturday 21 was called off. In practice: anyone flying from Malpensa between 1 and 5 pm checks the flight status with the airline before setting off; anyone leaving from Venice arrives at security with plenty of time to spare.
  • Trains stopped from 9 pm on Thursday 3 to 9 pm on Friday 4 December for the general strike by CUB, SGB and SI Cobas; motorways from 10 pm on the 3rd to 10 pm on the 4th (Source: Qualitytravel, 17 November; Telepass Moveo, 16 November)
    The Ministry's calendar lists for Friday 4 December a general strike of all public and private sectors called by CUB, ADL Varese, SGB, SBM, SGC and SI Cobas. For the railways the stoppage runs from 9 pm on Thursday 3 December to 9 pm on Friday 4 December; for motorways from 10 pm on the 3rd to 10 pm on the 4th; local public transport stops with arrangements and guaranteed bands decided city by city, still to be published, and for air transport the arrangements have not been announced. Also in December: on Monday 14 a 24-hour strike by Aviation Services staff at Rome Fiumicino and Ciampino, on Tuesday 15 a 24-hour stoppage by ENAV controllers at Bologna airport and a national taxi strike from 8 am to 10 pm. Anyone planning a train journey around the Immacolata long weekend chooses other dates if possible, or prepares to use the guaranteed trains that Trenitalia and Italo will publish in the days before.
  • Skiing: on Saturday 28 November Livigno, Courmayeur, La Thuile, Monterosa, Tonale and Plan de Corones open; on 4 December Bormio, Sestriere, Alta Badia and Val Gardena (Source: Sky TG24, 10 November; Funivie.it, updated 22 September; Scimarche, 28 September)
    According to the Sky TG24 calendar of 10 November, Breuil-Cervinia has been skiing since 24 October and Madonna di Campiglio opened on Saturday 21 November, snow permitting. On Friday 27 Obereggen and Madesimo start; on Saturday 28 November Livigno (lifts from 8 am), Courmayeur, La Thuile, the Monterosa Ski area of Champoluc, Alagna and Gressoney, Pontedilegno-Tonale, Plan de Corones, Alpe Cermis, Pampeago, San Martino di Castrozza and Passo San Pellegrino; on Sunday 29 Valtournenche. On 3 December it is the turn of Folgarida, Marilleva and Pinzolo, on Friday 4 December of Bormio, Sestriere and Vialattea, Alta Badia, Val Gardena, Alpe di Siusi, Val di Fassa, Arabba-Marmolada and Monte Bondone, on Saturday 5 of Antagnod, Brusson, Champorcher and Gressoney-Saint-Jean. Cortina, Abetone and Roccaraso do not yet have an official date. The Dolomiti Superski high-season day pass costs 89 euros, three more than last year; at Livigno the early-season promotional day pass is 51.50 euros, and from 28 November to 19 December guests staying at least 5 nights in a hotel booked directly get the ski pass included. Dates depend on snow cover: before leaving, check the ski area's website.
