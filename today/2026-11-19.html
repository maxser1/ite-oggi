Italy today · Thursday 19 November 2026

Yellow alert in eleven regions, Vietri sul Mare, the rail strike and Christmas in Trento

A Thursday of wind and rain with yellow alerts in eleven regions, the town of the day at the start of the Amalfi Coast, the feast of San Colombano in Bobbio on the 23rd, the 23-hour rail strike from 21.00 on Friday 20 to 20.00 on Saturday 21 and Trento's Christmas market opening on Friday 20.

The third week of November is the time for art cities without queues and the first Christmas markets, with the ski slopes still closed for another week. Today, though, the weather rules: a yellow alert for the whole of Umbria and Campania and parts of nine other regions, strong to gale-force winds and heavy sea storms on exposed coasts. Ferries, cable cars and coastal paths need checking.

No Italian town celebrates its patron saint today. The town of the day is Vietri sul Mare, in the province of Salerno, the easternmost on the Amalfi Coast: the centre sits 80 metres above the Marina, the station is 200 metres away and the name is bound to ceramics, with the Solimene factory designed by Paolo Soleri and the museum in Raito. The nearest festival is San Colombano in Bobbio, Monday 23 November.

The most useful news concerns trains: from 21.00 on Friday 20 to 20.00 on Saturday 21 a 23-hour national strike, with guaranteed windows from 6.00 to 9.00 and 18.00 to 21.00 for regional services. Anyone travelling on Saturday picks early morning or evening. From Friday 20 the 75 huts of Trento's Christmas market open.

Useful news for travellers

  • Yellow alert today in eleven regions, winds up to gale force from north to south and heavy sea storms on exposed coasts (Source: MeteoWeb, 18 November, 16.21)
    For Thursday 19 November the Civil Protection has issued a yellow alert for the whole of Umbria and Campania and for parts of Liguria, Emilia-Romagna, Tuscany, Marche, Lazio, Abruzzo, Molise, Basilicata and Calabria. The severe weather warning forecasts strong to gale-force westerly winds over Piedmont, Lombardy, Liguria, Emilia-Romagna, Tuscany, Marche, Umbria, Lazio, Abruzzo and Molise, extending to Campania, Puglia, Basilicata and Calabria, with gusts up to severe gale on Alpine and Apennine ridges and heavy sea storms along exposed coasts. The national weather bulletin indicates scattered to widespread rain, including showers and thunderstorms, over eastern Liguria, Tuscany, the Emilian Apennines, southern Umbria, inland Lazio, western Abruzzo and Molise, inland and northern Campania, Basilicata and northern Tyrrhenian Calabria; snow on the Alps above 1,400 to 1,600 metres, dropping to 1,000 to 1,200 metres in South Tyrol and Friuli Venezia Giulia; the Ligurian Sea rough to very rough. No orange alert.
  • Trains: 23-hour national strike from 21.00 on Friday 20 to 20.00 on Saturday 21 November, guaranteed windows 6.00-9.00 and 18.00-21.00 for regional services (Source: Qualitytravel.it, 16 November)
    From 21.00 on Friday 20 November to 20.00 on Saturday 21 the staff of the Ferrovie dello Stato group, Trenitalia, Trenitalia Tper and Trenord strike for 23 hours; Italo also stops. The heaviest impact is expected on Saturday 21, when the action affects almost the entire service. Trenitalia regional trains follow the usual weekday guaranteed windows, 6.00 to 9.00 and 18.00 to 21.00, but not every train in those windows is assured. On Friday Trenord runs trains departing by 21.00 and arriving by 22.00; on Saturday it guarantees the services on its minimum-service list departing after 6.00 and arriving by 9.00, or departing after 18.00 and arriving by 21.00, and if Malpensa Express trains are cancelled it runs non-stop buses from Milano Cadorna (via Paleocapa 1) and from Stabio. Italo and Trenitalia publish the lists of guaranteed long-distance trains. Friday's general strike, called by Cgil, covers several public and private sectors but excludes rail and air transport.
  • Trento opens its Christmas market on Friday 20 November: 75 huts in Piazza Fiera and Piazza Mostra until 6 January (Source: SiViaggia, 7 November)
    The Trento Christmas Market 2026 runs from 20 November to 6 January 2027, with 75 wooden huts in two squares: Piazza Fiera, along the medieval walls, and Piazza Mostra, at the foot of the Castello del Buonconsiglio and next to the Giardino Magico di San Marco, with crafts, decorations and local food. The city also brings back the big tree in Piazza Duomo, the ice rink in Piazza Battisti, the Piazza dei Bambini with a Ferris wheel in Piazza Dante and the Christmas Garden inside the Castello del Buonconsiglio. Elsewhere in Trentino: Pergine Valsugana from 14 November to 27 December, Levico Terme in the Habsburg park from 21 November with more than 50 huts, Lavarone from 28 November to 6 January, Forte di Tenna on 5-6 and 12-13 December. Opening hours and the lighting ceremony should be checked on the city's official website.
