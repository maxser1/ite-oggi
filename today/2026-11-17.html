Italy today · Tuesday 17 November 2026

Orange alert in Tuscany, Marano di Napoli and trains at a standstill from 9 pm on Friday 20

A Tuesday of heavy rain in Tuscany and storms in the South, the town of the day on the Camaldoli hill, the feast of San Colombano in Bobbio on the 23rd, the 23-hour rail strike between the 20th and 21st and Trento's Christmas market opening on Friday.

Mid-November is the quietest time of the year for visiting Italy: museums without queues, low hotel prices. The price is the weather: today a new Atlantic front crosses the country in twenty-four hours, with an orange alert over northern Tuscany and a yellow alert in five other regions. A day for indoors, with a look at the regional bulletin before setting out.

The calendar remembers Saint Gregory the Wonderworker. The town of the day is Marano di Napoli, a large hill town north-west of Naples, partly on the Camaldoli hill: it keeps the Castello di Monteleone, a hunting lodge built for Frederick II and rebuilt by Charles I of Anjou in 1275. The nearest feast is San Colombano in Bobbio, on Monday 23 November.

The most useful news: trains stop from 9 pm on Friday 20 to 8 pm on Saturday 21 November, for Trenitalia, Italo and Trenord. Anyone who has to travel on Saturday checks the lists of guaranteed trains from today and, if possible, moves the trip to Friday afternoon or Sunday.

Saint of the day

Today is the feast of Saint Gregory the Wonderworker, patron saint of 4 municipalities: Laureana di Borrello, Robassomero, Stalettì and Bozzole.

Useful news for travellers

  • Weather alert today: orange over northern Tuscany, yellow in Campania, Emilia-Romagna, Liguria, Tuscany, Umbria and Aosta Valley (Source: Fanpage.it, 16 November, 4.02 pm)
    For Tuesday 17 November the Civil Protection has issued an orange alert over northern Tuscany and a yellow alert in six regions: Campania, Emilia-Romagna, Liguria, Tuscany, Umbria and Aosta Valley. A new Atlantic front crosses Italy in about twenty-four hours with widespread and at times intense weather: in the North very cloudy skies with scattered rain and showers, first over Aosta Valley, Piedmont and Liguria, then Lombardy and the Triveneto; in the Centre intense weather in Tuscany and widespread rain over Sardinia, Umbria, Lazio and the western parts of Abruzzo, Molise and the Marche; in the South storms over Campania, western Basilicata and northern Calabria, spreading to the Gargano in the evening.
  • 23-hour rail strike from 9 pm on Friday 20 to 8 pm on Saturday 21 November: Trenitalia, Italo and Trenord (Source: Qualitytravel.it, 16 November; Fanpage.it, 16 November, 10.44 am)
    The national rail strike starts at 9 pm on Friday 20 November and ends at 8 pm on Saturday 21, with possible cancellations and delays all day Saturday and a gradual return to normal from 8 pm. Italo, Trenitalia, Trenitalia Tper and Trenord are involved; for Trenord the strike is called by CUB Trasporti, CAT and SGB. Trenitalia regional trains run in the guaranteed bands from 6 to 9 am and from 6 to 9 pm, limited to the trains in the official lists; Trenord runs on Friday the trains departing by 9 pm and arriving by 10 pm, on Saturday those in the 6-9 am and 6-9 pm bands on the minimum-service lists, and if the Malpensa Express is cancelled it provides non-stop buses from Milano Cadorna (via Paleocapa 1) and from Stabio. Italo and Trenitalia publish the lists of guaranteed long-distance trains. The 24-hour CGIL general strike on Friday 20 covers public and private sectors and local public transport, but not air or rail transport.
  • Trento: the Christmas market opens on Friday 20 November and runs until 6 January (Source: SiViaggia, 7 November, 9.00 am)
    Trento's Christmas market runs from 20 November 2026 to 6 January 2027 with 75 wooden stalls in two squares: Piazza Fiera, along the medieval walls, and Piazza Mostra, at the foot of the Castello del Buonconsiglio, next to the Giardino Magico di San Marco. The city also sets up the tree in Piazza Duomo, the children's square with a ferris wheel in Piazza Dante and the ice rink in Piazza Battisti. Mind Saturday 21: it coincides with the rail strike.
